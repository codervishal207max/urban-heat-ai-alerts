Platform Capabilities

FastAPI backend → ML models → Real-time risk engine → Interactive dashboard.

🛰️

Satellite Heat Mapping

Landsat-8 / Sentinel-2 LST & NDVI processing. 30×30 grid per city with UHI intensity scoring.

🤖

AI Risk Prediction

Random Forest / XGBoost on LST, NDVI, population density. Heat Vulnerability Index (HVI) per zone.

🏥

Health Impact Assessment

Estimated deaths, hospital beds needed, population at risk — computed live from backend ML service.

🌳

Planning Recommendations

Rule-based engine: green cover, cool roofs, pavements, cooling centers per zone priority.

🧪

What-If Simulation

Backend simulate API: "+20% green cover” → real LST reduction & health impact projection.

🗺️

Multi-City Dashboard

Switch between 10 major Indian cities. Backend serves per-city GeoJSON, frontend renders on Leaflet map.

💬

AI Chatbot (HeatBot)

Keyword-based assistant with Hindi+English support. Answers heat, health, climate, recommendation queries.

🗄️

Activity Database

Every user interaction — city switch, simulation, chat — logged and viewable with CSV export.

🌍

Climate Alert System

Rotating banner of real IPCC + India climate change alerts. Keeps users informed of urgency.
